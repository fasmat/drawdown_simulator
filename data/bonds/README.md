# Bonds and Interest Rates

- `10y_us_treasury.csv`: Historical yields of the 10-year US Treasury bond
   Source: [FRED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/DGS10)
- `geldmarkt.csv`: Historical yields of short-term money market instruments (e.g. Tagesgeld).
   Source: [Bundesbank](https://www.bundesbank.de/resource/blob/844248/5d555835809fed2b4b2abbb18e5c10ca/472B63F073F071307366337C94F8C870/i-3-geld-und-kapitalmarkt-data.pdf)
